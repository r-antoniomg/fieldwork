**Fieldwork** is an inquiry-driven practice that explores learning through mindful movement, making, and other forms of lived creative experimentation, from paper to mat to wherever it unfolds.

This site is an attempt to bring together a diverse range of interests that may otherwise be difficult to represent in terms of a more traditional brand, with a single focus and specific products or services to offer. Fieldwork prioritizes curiosity and exploration. Through fieldwork, I am open to collaborate on projects with other creators and brands. Occasionally I may have products or services to offer as well, but this brand is less about visitors as consumers, and more about visitors as participants.

Site navigation is intentionally non-linear, although some consistent structure has been created:
- Manifestation pages explore fieldwork practice from different prespectives: inquiry, movement, making, reflection.
  - Projects are discrete examples of completed or ongoing practice focused around a specific question or topic
  - Field notes are 'blog posts' and other musings that inform practice
  - Objects are for the most part images and artwork
