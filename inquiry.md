## Practice statement
As the driving force of fieldwork practice, inquiry involves curiosity and effort to understand and challenge assumptions.
It is not just an intellectual endeavour but a joyful iterative process of testing and experimentation.
It is less about having the 'correct answer' and more about coming up with interesting questions to guide exploration.

In my practice, inquiry is a mix between structure and flexibility: it is the place where exploration begins.
Sometimes, it will result in a new practice, sometimes it will be a one-off test.
Inquiry sparks from the things that I know (for example, librarianship) and expands into the edges of my comfort zone (for example, computer programming).
